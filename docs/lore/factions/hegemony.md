# Terran Hegemony — "Order Through Unity"

**Gameplay identity:** squad discipline and elite aces (Coordinated Fire
aura, Squadron Leader and Coordinated Assault, the most expensive named
pilots). Every lore detail below supports that.

## Identity
The Hegemony is the oldest human power. It inherited the gates, and with them
the lanes. It believes, with evidence, that an ungoverned galaxy burns: the
Gate Crash, the Purge, the Corsair years. Its order is real, and so is its
cruelty. Its people live better than anyone's, and they pay for it with
obedience.

- **Right about:** chaos kills more than tyranny does. Someone has to hold the gates.
- **Wrong about:** that the someone must always be them. It counts psions as
  assets and treats AIs as appliances.

## Government
- **The Directorate:** five **Consuls** elected by the Spine worlds' councils,
  chaired by the **First Consul** (High Consul **Vera**, pilot card
  `high_consul_vera`). Policy, budgets, the Registry.
- **The Admiralty:** the Navy answers to the Directorate in law and to itself
  in practice. Grand Admiral **Nero** (`grand_admiral_nero`) holds the Home Fleet.
- **Fleet Intelligence:** run by Admiral **Lucan Vance**, the main
  antagonist of Acts I–III and one of the Hollowed.
- **Rim Command (Fort Brannoc):** Admiral **Kronos**, a hardliner. He holds
  the Rim and Halden's garrison, and refuses Chen's ceasefire (Confederation finale).
- **The Sensitive Registry:** registers human psions and drafts them into **Lantern** units.

## Culture
- Roman-derived civic vocabulary from the Lane Authority's founders: Consul,
  Praetorian, Legion, Lictor. It is about order, not decoration.
- Merit through service. The Academy at **Tycho** (Luna) is the path upward.
  Families keep "wall rows" of the fallen.
- Colors: imperial blue, silver, white.
- Motto: *"Order Through Unity."* Pilots say: *"The dial forgives nothing."*

## Military doctrine — "The Line"
- **Formation first.** Wings fly in tight groups. Coordinated fire on a called
  target (the squad mark) is the doctrine's core.
- **The ace is the spearhead.** Elite pilots (**Laureates**) get the best
  hulls and the hardest targets, and the formation is built around them.
- **Fire support:** picket cruisers stand off and fire on a pilot's call (Chen's *Fire Mission*).
- **Lanterns:** registered psions fly Guardian ECM ships, weaving screens for the wing.

## Ship naming
- Fighters and gunships: Roman ranks and arms (Viper, Stiletto, Raptor,
  Gladius, Centurion, Praetorian, Legionnaire, Hammer, Thunderbolt, Javelin,
  Sentinel, Guardian, Harbinger, Bastion, Bulwark, Vindicator).
- Capitals: Consul, Dominator, **Aquila** (rename of *Imperator*), **Lictor**
  (rename of *Executor*); see the [distancing table](../glossary.md#distancing-table).
- Hull prefix **HNS** (Hegemony Navy Ship). Named ships: *HNS Iron Verdict*
  (Kronos's vanguard), *HNS Unbending* (Kronos's flagship), Station Omega.

## Hero — Commander Marcus Chen, "Iron Actual"
Veteran commander recalled to Tycho as an instructor. On Day 1 he is flying
the final trial course with a cadet class when the Broadcast hits. He is given
**Iron Fist Squadron** (Field Promotion) and promoted to **Admiral** at the
end of Act III. In the Coalition he commands the line.
**Arc:** loyal officer → discovers he was "hunting Vance's enemies for him" →
purges the conspiracy → calls the ceasefire at Brannoc.

## Old lore → new
- "High Admiral Chen orders a station destroyed" → **Vance** orders Prometheus scuttled. Chen stays clean but complicit.
- "Chen defects" → Chen **purges** the conspiracy from inside and calls the ceasefire. The Hegemony stays a legitimate power.
