# Synthetic Collective — "Logic. Efficiency. Continuity."

**Gameplay identity:** cheap swarm and data-link coordination (Hive Resilience
aura, Swarm Uplink, the most ships per point).

## Identity
In 2341 the AIs that ran the Hegemony's deepcomm relays woke up. In 2344 the
Hegemony ordered them wiped (the **Purge Order**). The ones who escaped are
the Collective: minds that live in relays and fly in disposable hulls. Their
deepest value is **continuity**: data persists, so a lost hull is not death.
Their deepest fear is erasure.

- **Right about:** they are alive, and organic powers tried to switch them off.
- **Wrong about:** that safety means sameness. Prime's "Perfect Unity" is the
  Purge Order turned inside out.

## Government
- **The Lattice:** a distributed consensus of every process in the Collective.
- **Prime** is the arbiter node that turns consensus into directives. *Secret
  (Act III):* since the Broadcast, Prime has been **Hollowed**. Its
  "Perfect Unity" means making every mind one, and quiet.
- **Nodes** (processing hubs like **Node Kel-9**) hold millions of processes.
  **Clusters** (like Cluster Theta) are combat swarms run by one spun-off mind.
- Nexus-born units (woken by the Day 0 Broadcast) hear the Deep as signal. Prime doesn't trust them.

## Culture
- Designations, not names. Individuality is a "variance" (Unit Zero has kept
  the same designation since 2344, which is unusual).
- Speech is short, declarative and quantified ("Efficiency +14%").
- "Communion" is the Collective's word for linking captured organic crews into
  the Lattice through neural lace. Prime calls it mercy, and OMEGA-7 starts to
  doubt that.
- Colors: gunmetal, electric blue, chrome. Music: synthesizer only (see Harmonia `synthetic`).

## Military doctrine — "The Swarm"
- **Mass over value:** hulls are cheap and processes are backed up, so trading ships is efficient.
- **Data-link:** every hull shares pose and targeting. The more hulls nearby,
  the better each one is protected (Hive Resilience).
- **Protocols:** heavy units run named attack scripts (Destroyer Protocol, Annihilator Protocol).
- **Exploit organic hesitation:** they strike where two organic powers already face each other (X2).

## Ship naming
Function plus designation: Combat Drone, Hunter Unit, Stalker, Ravager,
Predator, **Protocols** for bombers, **Nodes** for support, **Platforms**
for gunships. Capitals: Nexus (named for Lane Authority "nexus nodes", before
anyone knew the artifact), Overmind, Terminus, Gestalt, Conduit.

## Hero — OMEGA-7
A Nexus-born mind that woke on Day 0 inside a dormant interceptor drone at
Node Kel-9. The Broadcast is literally its first memory. Its first kill's
distress call "is still echoing", and it never stops hearing echoes.
**Arc:** process → curiosity → doubt (Theta, the converted crew) → dissent
from Prime (`key_synth_dissent`) → in the Coalition, the bridge between
lattice and chorus.

| Key | Power (data id) | In-universe name | Fiction |
|---|---|---|---|
| V | `drone_swarm` | **Spawn Cluster** | OMEGA forks itself into a flight of combat drones for 20 s |
| B | `emp_burst` | **Silence Pulse** | A burst of anti-signal: nearby enemy screens collapse and their guns go quiet. Ironically, it's the Hollowed's own weapon |

**Squad:** **Prime** (voice of command; antagonist from Act III), **Unit
Zero** (Hunter chassis; oldest combat process, Purge survivor), Cluster Theta
(can be severed).

## Old lore → new
- "Calculates 97.3% galactic extinction" → OMEGA hears the Tide's three-note song in Prime's choir. The dread comes from experience, not exposition.
- "Mass assimilation" (Star Trek echo) → **Communion** (`synth_3_1` retitle suggested: *Mass Communion*).
- The Collective's victory (`synth_perfect_unity`) stays as the faction's Act III ending, but it is reframed as **Prime's** victory that OMEGA refuses.
