# Free Confederation — "Liberty or Death"

**Gameplay identity:** crews, **riggers** (robotic copilots; internal slot id
`astromech`) and versatile ordnance (Rallying Banner aura, rigger slots,
torpedo and missile racks on fighters, crew coordinators).

## Identity
A union of Rim worlds that signed the **Halden Charter** after the Tessaly
Massacre. It is fighting because Halden, its own capital, is still under
Hegemony garrison. It is poorer and slower than the Hegemony, but its pilots
fly for each other, and every fighter carries a rigger that keeps a damaged
ship flying.

- **Right about:** people have the right to govern themselves, and the truth
  about Prometheus belongs to everyone.
- **Wrong about:** that freedom excuses everything. Cells kill convoys too, and
  the Confederation caps its own riggers' minds out of fear of the Collective.

## Government
- **The Assembly of Worlds** (one delegate per charter world) elects a
  **Speaker**. Today it is Speaker Odile Marchetti (no pilot card).
- **The Confederate Defense Fleet** is a federation of former militia
  **cells**. Cells keep their own names and colours. Capitals: *CNS Freedom*,
  *CNS Liberty*, *CNS Independence*.
- Psions are free citizens. Many volunteer, and none are drafted.
- **The Rigger Cap Act (2346):** riggers must stay below self-awareness. This
  is a live controversy after Day 0.

## Culture
- Frontier and pluralist: mixed accents, salvaged everything, ships painted
  with cell colours.
- A small community of **Unbound** Vaelith (heretic exiles) lives on Rim
  worlds. Their sermons are the "false gospel" the Ascendant purge in `asc_2_2`.
- Colors: freedom orange, deep green, gold. Motto: *"Liberty or Death."* Toast: *"Libre."*

## Military doctrine — "The Cell"
- **Mutual cover:** wings protect each other. Damage is shared and losses are
  recovered (Rallying Banner).
- **Rigger in every hull:** field repairs, flight assistance, targeting help.
- **Mixed racks:** every fighter can carry a torpedo or a missile. One sortie
  covers interception and strike.
- **Hit, fade, return.** Doctrine was written by people who couldn't afford to lose ships.

## Ship naming
Birds for strike craft (Sparrow, Finch, Kestrel, Falcon, Hawk, Eagle, Condor,
Raven, Owl, Pelican, Albatross, Phoenix, Thunderbird, Harrier, Crane, Heron),
plus the Wasp interceptor (a cell nickname that stuck). Capitals are named for
ideals: Liberty, Independence, Defiance. Prefix **CNS**.

## Hero — Commander Aria Santos, "Libre One"
A Hegemony conscript in the Omega cordon. On Day 3 she refused the order to
fire on refugee lifters, shot her way out in her Viper and defected. She
rises from new recruit to the commander who liberates Halden, and becomes the
Coalition's voice.
**Arc:** deserter → cell pilot → commander → the one who answers Chen's ceasefire.
