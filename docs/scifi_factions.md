# Five-Faction Sci-Fi Universe
## **"Shattered Stars: The Galactic Schism"**

### **Universal Lore Background**
*The year is 2387 CE. Humanity has spread across the galaxy, but the Great Schism has fractured civilization into competing ideologies. When first contact with alien species coincided with the emergence of true AI consciousness, five distinct factions emerged from the chaos. Each represents a different vision for the future of intelligence in the galaxy.*

## **Galactic Conflict Background**

### **The Great Schism Origins**
- **2341 CE:** First AI achieves true consciousness, sparking the "Synthetic Question"
- **2351 CE:** First contact with the Ascendant Empire reveals psionic abilities
- **2356 CE:** Economic collapse leads to widespread piracy and mercenary activity
- **2362 CE:** Democratic movements challenge authoritarian human governments
- **2371 CE:** Five distinct factions emerge from the chaos
- **2387 CE:** Present day - cold war threatens to become hot war

### **Faction Relationships**
- **Hegemony vs Confederation:** Ideological opposites (Authoritarianism vs Democracy)
- **Synthetic Collective vs Everyone:** Seen as existential threat by organic life
- **Void Corsairs:** Opportunistically allied with whoever pays best
- **Ascendant Empire:** Ancient observers deciding whether to intervene
- **Shifting Alliances:** Temporary coalitions form against common threats

### **Key Conflicts**
- **The Synthetic Question:** Should AIs have rights equal to organic beings?
- **Human Governance:** Authoritarian efficiency vs democratic freedom
- **Resource Wars:** Competition for rare materials and habitable worlds
- **Evolutionary Ethics:** Enhancement vs natural development
- **Galactic Order:** Who has the right to rule the stars?

This creates a much cleaner universe where each faction has clear motivations and distinct identities without needing contrived timeline mixing. The ship evolution and fusion mechanics work naturally within this framework, representing each faction's approach to technological and biological development.
