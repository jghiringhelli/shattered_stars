## **ART STYLE REFERENCE**

### **Core Visual Philosophy**
*Inspired by Chris Metzen's Warcraft 2, StarCraft 1, and Disciples 2 aesthetic*

**Key Style Elements:**
- **Bold, chunky silhouettes** with strong contrast and readability
- **Rich, saturated colors** with clear faction identity
- **Mechanical detail** balanced with clean overall design
- **Progressive evolution** showing clear advancement tiers
- **Iconic, memorable shapes** that read well at small sizes

**Base Style Prompt for All Ships:**
```
"Top-down spaceship sprite, Chris Metzen art style, chunky mechanical design, 
bold silhouette, rich saturated colors, StarCraft aesthetic, pixel art inspired, 
high contrast, game sprite quality, detailed but readable at small scale"
```

COMPONENT BREAKDOWN:
- Hunter Drone → Right Arm (weapons)
- Interceptor Drone → Left Arm (speed)
- Devastator Bomber → Torso (heavy weapons)
- Guardian Fighter → Head (command)
- Support Drone → Legs (stability)

ASSEMBLY SEQUENCE SPRITES:
"Five AI ships approaching combination point, blue energy fields 
connecting, transformation sequence, mechanical precision, 
Metzen-style mecha transformation --ar 2:1"

COMBINED ABILITIES:
- All component ship abilities active
- Massive size and firepower
- Limited duration (5 turns)
- Vulnerable during assembly/separation
