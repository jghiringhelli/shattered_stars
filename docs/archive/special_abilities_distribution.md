# Special Abilities Distribution & Role-Based Ship Design
## **Thematically Distributed Special Mechanics Across All Factions**

---

## **ROLE-BASED SHIP MATRIX**

### **Core Combat Roles**
Each faction needs representation in these essential roles:
- **Light Fighter** (Fast, agile, basic combat)
- **Heavy Fighter** (Balanced, versatile, mainline combat)
- **Interceptor** (Ultra-fast, fragile, arc-dodging)
- **Bomber** (Heavy ordnance, anti-capital)
- **Support** (Coordination, healing, utility)
- **Gunship** (Heavy weapons, durability)
- **Capital** (Command, multi-section, epic encounters)

## **SPECIAL ABILITIES THEMATIC DISTRIBUTION**

### **CLOAKING TECHNOLOGY**
*"Stealth and surprise attacks"*

**Terran Hegemony: Military Stealth**
- **Phantom Recon Fighter** *(TIE Phantom stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Tactical Cloak" - Standard cloak, +1 defense when decloaking to attack
  - **Evolution:** Hunter Drone (Collective) → Phantom Recon (defected/captured tech)
  - **Role:** Interceptor/Scout

**Void Corsairs: Pirate Stealth**
- **Ghost-class Raider** *(Quadjumper base, enhanced)*
  - **Ability:** "Smuggler's Cloak" - Can decloak and attack same turn, but only at Range 1
  - **Evolution:** Dart Scout → Ghost Raider (illegal stealth mods)
  - **Role:** Light Fighter/Infiltrator

**Synthetic Collective: Digital Stealth**
- **Wraith Protocol Drone** *(M3-A base, enhanced)*
  - **Ability:** "Phase Shift" - Cloak using Calculate action, immune to stress while cloaked
  - **Evolution:** Scout Probe → Wraith Drone (advanced AI protocols)
  - **Role:** Interceptor/Electronic Warfare

### **SLAM TECHNOLOGY**
*"Extended movement and strategic positioning"*

**Free Confederation: Strategic Mobility**
- **Tornado Strategic Bomber** *(K-Wing stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Combat SLAM" - SLAM + attack in same turn, +1 torpedo damage after SLAM
  - **Evolution:** Meteor Bomber → Tornado Bomber (rebellion innovation)
  - **Role:** Heavy Bomber/Strategic Strike

**Synthetic Collective: Drone Protocols**
- **Rapid Response Drone** *(Alpha Star Wing base)*
  - **Ability:** "Protocol SLAM" - SLAM without stress, must move straight line
  - **Evolution:** Guardian Fighter → Rapid Response (efficiency protocols)
  - **Role:** Support/Rapid Deployment

**Ascendant Empire: Bio-Enhancement**
- **Velocity Symbiont** *(TIE Striker base, evolved)*
  - **Ability:** "Bio-Boost" - SLAM heals 1 hull, can change direction mid-SLAM
  - **Evolution:** Metamorph Striker → Velocity Symbiont (living engines)
  - **Role:** Heavy Fighter/Bio-Enhanced

### **ROTATING ARCS**
*"Flexible weapon positioning"*

**Terran Hegemony: Military Engineering**
- **Siege Platform** *(B-Wing stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Tactical Rotation" - Rotate arc, +1 attack when arc targets multiple enemies
  - **Evolution:** Devastator Bomber → Siege Platform (heavy weapons platform)
  - **Role:** Gunship/Anti-Capital

**Free Confederation: Adaptive Design**
- **Comet Striker** *(B-Wing stats, Confederation colors)*
  - **Ability:** "Rebel Engineering" - Rotate arc, can barrel roll after rotating
  - **Evolution:** Meteor Bomber → Comet Striker (field modification)
  - **Role:** Heavy Fighter/Assault

**Synthetic Collective: Automated Targeting**
- **Titan Destroyer** *(Aggressor stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Auto-Targeting" - Rotate arc automatically when acquiring locks
  - **Evolution:** Heavy Drone → Titan Destroyer (weapon automation)
  - **Role:** Gunship/Heavy Assault

**Ascendant Empire: Bio-Mechanical**
- **Mutation Assault Form** *(B-Wing base, bio-enhanced)*
  - **Ability:** "Living Weapons" - Rotate arc, regenerate 1 shield when rotating
  - **Evolution:** Plague Bomber → Mutation Assault (bio-weapon evolution)
  - **Role:** Heavy Fighter/Bio-Weapon

### **COORDINATE ACTION**
*"Leadership and tactical support"*

**Terran Hegemony: Command Structure**
- **Command Shuttle** *(Lambda stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Fleet Command" - Coordinate Range 1-3, targets gain formation bonuses
  - **Role:** Support/Command

- **Reaper Support Craft** *(TIE Reaper stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Squadron Leader" - Coordinate grants additional action to fighters
  - **Evolution:** Devastator Bomber → Reaper Support (command conversion)
  - **Role:** Support/Squadron Command

**Free Confederation: Inspirational Leadership**
- **Atlas Command Transport** *(U-Wing stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Hero's Rally" - Coordinate grants heroic inspiration bonuses
  - **Role:** Support/Transport

- **Liberty Command Ship** *(VCX-100 stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Resistance Cell" - Coordinate through obstacles, unlimited range to small ships
  - **Role:** Support/Mobile Command

**Synthetic Collective: Network Coordination**
- **Control Node** *(G-1A stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Network Command" - Coordinate unlimited range to same ship type
  - **Role:** Support/AI Coordinator

- **Relay Command Ship** *(Attack Shuttle stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Data Link" - Coordinate grants calculate tokens instead of actions
  - **Evolution:** Coordinator Support → Relay Command (network upgrade)
  - **Role:** Support/Network Node

**Ascendant Empire: Psionic Leadership**
- **Thought Coordinator** *(TIE Reaper base, psionic)*
  - **Ability:** "Mind Link" - Coordinate ignores range and obstacles
  - **Evolution:** Cerebral Command → Thought Coordinator (psionic mastery)
  - **Role:** Support/Psionic Command

### **JAM ACTION**
*"Electronic warfare and disruption"*

**Terran Hegemony: Electronic Warfare**
- **Hawk Electronic Fighter** *(TIE Striker base, EW variant)*
  - **Ability:** "Military Jamming" - Jam removes all tokens, +1 damage if target can't spend tokens
  - **Evolution:** Hawk Fighter → Electronic Variant (specialized training)
  - **Role:** Interceptor/Electronic Warfare

**Synthetic Collective: Digital Warfare**
- **Disruptor Drone** *(Vulture base, enhanced)*
  - **Ability:** "System Override" - Jam causes stress, spreads to Range 1 ships
  - **Evolution:** Hunter Drone → Disruptor (cyber warfare protocols)
  - **Role:** Light Fighter/Cyber Warfare

**Void Corsairs: Pirate Tech**
- **Havoc Jammer** *(Kimogila base, modified)*
  - **Ability:** "Chaos Field" - Jam affects all ships at Range 1-2, including friendlies
  - **Evolution:** Havoc Gunship → Jammer Variant (stolen military tech)
  - **Role:** Gunship/Area Denial

### **BOOST & BARREL ROLL COMBINATIONS**
*"Advanced maneuverability"*

**Free Confederation: Pilot Skill**
- **Vortex Interceptor** *(A-Wing stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Ace Maneuvers" - Can boost AND barrel roll in same turn
  - **Evolution:** Comet Fighter → Vortex Interceptor (pilot excellence)
  - **Role:** Interceptor/Arc Dodger

**Void Corsairs: Pirate Agility**
- **Razor Raider** *(StarViper stats)*
  - **Ability:** "Dirty Dancing" - Boost/barrel roll after attacking from behind
  - **Evolution:** Scythe Fighter → Razor Raider (pirate modifications)
  - **Role:** Heavy Fighter/Hit-and-Run

**Ascendant Empire: Bio-Enhancement**
- **Adaptation Fighter** *(E-Wing base, bio-enhanced)*
  - **Ability:** "Evolutionary Flight" - Boost OR barrel roll heals 1 damage
  - **Evolution:** Hybrid Fighter → Adaptation Fighter (bio-perfection)
  - **Role:** Heavy Fighter/Self-Repair

## **THEMATIC JUSTIFICATIONS**

### **Why Each Faction Has Certain Abilities**

**Terran Hegemony:**
- **Cloak:** Military stealth technology for reconnaissance
- **Rotating Arc:** Military engineering for flexible weapons platforms
- **Coordinate:** Military command structure and discipline
- **Jam:** Electronic warfare capabilities

**Free Confederation:**
- **SLAM:** Innovative rebel engineering for strategic mobility
- **Rotating Arc:** Field modifications and adaptive engineering
- **Coordinate:** Inspiring leadership and heroic example
- **Advanced Maneuvers:** Superior pilot training and skill

**Synthetic Collective:**
- **Cloak:** Digital stealth and phase technology
- **SLAM:** Efficient AI protocols for rapid deployment
- **Rotating Arc:** Automated targeting systems
- **Coordinate:** Network communication and AI coordination
- **Jam:** Cyber warfare and system disruption

**Void Corsairs:**
- **Cloak:** Illegal stealth modifications for smuggling
- **Jam:** Jury-rigged disruption systems
- **Advanced Maneuvers:** Pirate agility and dirty fighting

**Ascendant Empire:**
- **SLAM:** Bio-enhanced propulsion systems
- **Rotating Arc:** Bio-mechanical weapon systems
- **Coordinate:** Psionic coordination abilities
- **Advanced Maneuvers:** Bio-enhanced reflexes and adaptation

This distribution ensures every faction has unique capabilities while maintaining thematic consistency. Each special ability feels natural to its faction's identity, and the role-based design ensures balanced gameplay across all faction matchups.
