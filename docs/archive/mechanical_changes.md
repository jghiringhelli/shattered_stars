## **FACTION SPECIAL ABILITIES**

### **TERRAN HEGEMONY** - "Disciplined Formation"
**Core Philosophy:** Military precision and coordinated tactics

**Faction Abilities:**
- **Formation Discipline:** Ships at Range 1 of 2+ friendly ships get +1 defense die
- **Tactical Coordination:** When target locking, may share the lock with friendly ships at Range 1-2
- **Shield Harmonics:** When a friendly ship at Range 1 recovers shields, this ship recovers 1 shield
- **Command Authority:** Large ships can spend their action to grant an action to all friendly ships at Range 1-3

**Ship Modifications:**
- **All Hegemony ships** gain +1 shield capacity (minimum 1)
- **Fighters** gain "Disciplined" keyword: +1 attack when 2+ friendlies at Range 1
- **Capital ships** gain "Command Node" ability: coordinate action range increased to 1-3

### **FREE CONFEDERATION** - "Adaptive Heroes"
**Core Philosophy:** Versatility, heroics, and scrappy determination

**Faction Abilities:**
- **Heroic Inspiration:** When outnumbered, all ships get +1 attack and +1 defense
- **Adaptive Systems:** Can change 1 upgrade between missions without penalty
- **Lucky Breaks:** Once per mission, may reroll any single die (attack, defense, or critical)
- **Jury-Rigged Repairs:** Can spend 2 actions to recover 1 hull point

**Ship Modifications:**
- **All Confederation ships** can equip 1 additional modification upgrade
- **Fighters** gain "Heroic" keyword: +1 attack when at less than half hull
- **Freighters** gain "Smuggler's Luck": ignore 1 damage from critical hits

### **SYNTHETIC COLLECTIVE** - "Networked Intelligence"
**Core Philosophy:** Calculated precision and hive mind coordination

**Faction Abilities:**
- **Network Protocol:** All ships use Calculate action instead of Focus (convert 1 die to hit/evade)
- **Collective Targeting:** When one ship target locks an enemy, all ships at Range 1-3 can use that lock
- **Auto-Repair Subroutines:** Recover 1 hull point at start of each turn if not stressed
- **Swarm Intelligence:** +1 attack die for each friendly ship beyond the first targeting same enemy

**Ship Modifications:**
- **All Collective ships** immune to stress from red maneuvers
- **Drones** gain "Expendable" keyword: when destroyed, deal 1 damage to enemy at Range 1
- **Command ships** gain "Control Node": can coordinate with unlimited range to drones

### **VOID CORSAIRS** - "Dirty Fighting"
**Core Philosophy:** Unpredictable tactics and underhanded tricks

**Faction Abilities:**
- **Dirty Tricks:** Critical hits deal +1 damage
- **Black Market Tech:** Can equip illegal upgrade combinations (2 restricted items)
- **Hit and Run:** After attacking, may perform free barrel roll or boost action
- **Salvage Operations:** Gain 1 resource token when an enemy ship is destroyed nearby (Range 1-2)

**Ship Modifications:**
- **All Corsair ships** gain +1 critical hit effect when attacking from behind
- **Raiders** gain "Pirate" keyword: +1 agility when not in enemy firing arc
- **Gunships** gain "Predator" keyword: +1 attack against damaged enemies

### **ASCENDANT EMPIRE** - "Bio-Tech Supremacy"
**Core Philosophy:** Evolution, regeneration, and psionic powers

**Faction Abilities:**
- **Bio-Regeneration:** Recover 1 shield at end of each turn (if not at max)
- **Psionic Coordination:** Ignore range restrictions for coordinate actions
- **Adaptive Evolution:** Once per mission, may increase any ship stat by 1 for remainder of battle
- **Symbiotic Systems:** When damaged, may transfer damage to friendly bio-ship at Range 1

**Ship Modifications:**
- **All Empire ships** gain 1 additional hull point
- **Bio-fighters** gain "Regeneration" keyword: heal 1 hull at start of turn if shields full
- **Psionic ships** gain "Mind Link" keyword: can coordinate through obstacles and beyond range

## **REDISTRIBUTED SPECIAL MECHANICS**

### **Cloaking Technology**
**Originally:** TIE Phantom only
**New Distribution:**
- **Terran Hegemony:** Phantom Stealth Fighter (evolved from advanced fighters)
- **Void Corsairs:** Ghost Infiltrator (stealth raider variant)
- **Synthetic Collective:** Wraith Protocol upgrade (experimental tech)

**Cloak Variants:**
- **Military Cloak (Hegemony):** Standard cloaking, +1 defense when decloaking
- **Pirate Cloak (Corsairs):** Can decloak and attack same turn, -1 defense
- **Digital Cloak (Collective):** Calculate action when cloaking, immune to stress

### **SLAM Technology** 
**Originally:** K-Wing only
**New Distribution:**
- **Free Confederation:** Tornado Bomber (evolved strategic bomber)
- **Synthetic Collective:** Multiple units (drones are SLAM-capable)
- **Ascendant Empire:** Virus Siege Ship (bio-mechanical enhancement)

**SLAM Variants:**
- **Strategic SLAM (Confederation):** Standard SLAM, +1 torpedo attack after SLAM
- **Protocol SLAM (Collective):** No stress penalty, but must move in straight line
- **Bio-SLAM (Empire):** SLAM movement heals 1 hull point from bio-enhancement

### **Rotating Weapon Arcs**
**Originally:** B-Wing only
**New Distribution:**
- **Terran Hegemony:** Siege Bomber (heavy weapons platform)
- **Free Confederation:** Comet Striker (evolved bomber)
- **Synthetic Collective:** Titan Destroyer (rotating weapon pods)
- **Ascendant Empire:** Mutation Assault Craft (bio-mechanical weapons)

### **Coordinate Action**
**Enhanced Distribution:**
- **Terran Hegemony:** Command ships (enhanced range and targets)
- **Synthetic Collective:** Control nodes (unlimited range to drones)
- **Ascendant Empire:** Psionic ships (ignore obstacles and range)
- **Free Confederation:** Support ships (grants inspiration bonuses)

## **BALANCE VALIDATION MATRIX**

### **Rock-Paper-Scissors Relationships**
```
Terran Hegemony > Synthetic Collective (formation beats swarm)
Synthetic Collective > Void Corsairs (numbers beat tricks)
Void Corsairs > Ascendant Empire (speed beats regen)
Ascendant Empire > Free Confederation (elite beats balanced)
Free Confederation > Terran Hegemony (adaptability beats rigidity)
```

### **Faction Efficiency Ratings**
**Against different target types:**
- **Swarm enemies:** Hegemony (formation fire) > Collective (networking) > others
- **Elite enemies:** Empire (regen tank) > Corsairs (dirty tricks) > others  
- **Mixed forces:** Confederation (adaptable) > others (depends on situation)
- **Capital ships:** Collective (focus fire) > Corsairs (critical hits) > others

### **Mission Type Advantages**
- **Escort missions:** Hegemony (formation protection)
- **Assassination:** Corsairs (dirty fighting, critical hits)
- **Attrition warfare:** Empire (regeneration) and Collective (auto-repair)
- **Objective missions:** Confederation (adaptability)
- **Large battles:** Collective (swarm coordination)
