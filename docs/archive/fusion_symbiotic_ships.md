## **FUSION & ASSEMBLY MECHANICS**

### **Core Combination Types**
1. **Mechanical Assembly:** Ships physically dock to form larger vessels
2. **Bio-Fusion:** Living ships merge into hybrid organisms  
3. **Digital Integration:** AI ships network into collective entities
4. **Parasitic Symbiosis:** Smaller ships attach to and enhance larger ones
5. **Temporary Combinations:** Short-term tactical mergers

### **Universal Combination Rules**
- **Setup Requirement:** Ships must start within Range 1 of each other
- **Assembly Action:** Requires coordinate action from one ship, acceptance from others
- **Assembly Time:** Takes 1 full round to complete (vulnerable during assembly)
- **Combined Stats:** Specific formulas based on combination type
- **Separation:** Can separate as an action, but may cause damage/stress

## **TERRAN HEGEMONY - "MODULAR ASSEMBLY SYSTEM"**
*Military engineering for flexible force projection*

### **Talon Squadron Assembly** *(3 Talon Fighters → Trident Formation)*
**Assembly Process:**
- **Requirements:** 3 Talon Fighters within Range 1
- **Assembly Action:** Lead pilot coordinates, others accept
- **Assembly Time:** 1 round (all ships vulnerable while linking)

**Combined Stats:**
- **Hull:** 8 (3+3+3-1 for structural stress)
- **Shields:** 6 (2+2+2)
- **Attack:** 4 (best ship +1 for synchronized fire)
- **Agility:** 2 (average of component ships)
- **Size:** Medium (combined mass)

**Special Abilities:**
- **Synchronized Strike:** Can attack 3 different targets in one action
- **Formation Protocols:** +1 defense when all components healthy
- **Tactical Separation:** Can split apart to flank enemy, then reassemble

**Separation Consequences:**
- **Emergency Separation:** All ships suffer 1 damage and stress
- **Planned Separation:** No penalties, ships gain +1 agility for 1 turn

## **VOID CORSAIRS - "PARASITIC ATTACHMENT SYSTEM"**
*Smaller ships latch onto larger ones for mutual benefit*

### **Raider Pack Symbiosis** *(3 Light Raiders + 1 Heavy Ship → Pirate Fleet)*
**Concept:** Small, fast ships attach to larger vessel as external pods

**Host Ship Types:**
- **Marauder Assault Ship** (Firespray-31 equivalent)
- **Predator Heavy Fighter** (YV-666 equivalent)
- **Any Large Corsair vessel**

**Attachment Process:**
- **Docking:** Light ships perform coordinate action to attach
- **Integration:** Attached ships share power and systems
- **Detachment:** Can launch as free action for surprise attacks

**Combined Benefits:**
**Host Ship Gains:**
- **+2 Attack** (additional weapon pods)
- **+360° Coverage** (attached ships provide all-around defense)
- **+Support Systems** (attached ships provide crew and specialists)

**Attached Ships Gain:**
- **+2 Hull** (protection from host ship's armor)
- **+1 Shield** (shared defensive systems)
- **+Free Movement** (move with host ship without spending actions)

**Special Tactics:**
- **Surprise Launch:** Attached ships can detach and attack in same turn
- **Boarding Action:** Light ships can attempt to board enemy vessels
- **Sacrificial Protection:** Attached ships can intercept attacks on host

### **Scavenger Symbiosis** *(Multiple Small Ships + Damaged Large Ship → Jury-Rigged Hybrid)*
**Concept:** Small ships cannibalize themselves to repair and enhance larger vessel

**Requirements:**
- **1 Damaged Large Ship** (less than 50% hull remaining)
- **2-3 Small Ships** willing to be cannibalized
- **Scavenger crews** with appropriate skills

**Symbiosis Process:**
- **Sacrifice:** Small ships are destroyed to provide parts
- **Integration:** Large ship gains components from sacrificed vessels
- **Enhancement:** Combined vessel gains hybrid capabilities

**Results:**
- **Hull Recovery:** +4 hull points from cannibalized materials
- **System Integration:** Gains actions/abilities from sacrificed ships
- **Jury-Rigged Bonuses:** +1 to stats but -1 reliability (more critical failures)
- **Unique Configuration:** One-of-a-kind ship with unpredictable capabilities

### **Parasite Fighter Swarm** *(6 Tiny Fighters + 1 Capital Ship → Carrier Configuration)*
**Concept:** Micro-fighters that dock inside larger ship's structure

**Parasite Fighter Stats:**
- **Hull:** 1 | **Shields:** 0 | **Attack:** 1 | **Agility:** 4
- **Size:** Tiny (smaller than small)
- **Special:** Can dock inside any friendly large ship

**Carrier Integration:**
- **Internal Docking:** Up to 6 parasite fighters can dock inside large ship
- **Launch Capability:** Can launch 1-2 fighters per turn as free action
- **Rapid Recovery:** Fighters can dock for repairs and rearming
- **Surprise Attacks:** Enemies don't know how many fighters are docked

**Tactical Applications:**
- **Scout Deployment:** Launch fighters for reconnaissance
- **Point Defense:** Intercept incoming missiles and torpedoes
- **Boarding Actions:** Fighters can attempt to board enemy ships
- **Kamikaze Strikes:** Expendable fighters for desperate attacks

**Fusion Requirements:**

**Fusion Results:**
- **Enhanced Stats:** Best of both ships +1 in all categories
- **Dual Consciousness:** Two pilot minds sharing one vessel
- **Bio-Adaptation:** Gains abilities from both original ship types
- **Regenerative Fusion:** Can heal damage by redistributing biological mass

**Examples:**
**Hybrid Fighter + Mind Fighter → Psionic Warrior**
- **Hull:** 5 (3+2) | **Shields:** 4 (2+2) | **Attack:** 4 (3+1) | **Agility:** 3 (2+1)

**Host Ship Benefits:**

**Symbiont Benefits:**
- **Protection:** Shares host's hull and shields

- **Size Advantage:** Counts as host ship's size for targeting

## **FUSION TACTICAL CONSIDERATIONS**

### **Strategic Applications**
**Formation Assembly:**
- **Early Game:** Combine weak ships for immediate power boost
- **Mid Game:** Tactical assembly for specific mission requirements
- **Late Game:** Combine elite ships for ultimate configurations

**Risk/Reward Balance:**
- **Vulnerability:** Ships vulnerable during assembly process
- **Commitment:** Combined ships can't easily separate
- **Power Spike:** Significant capability increase when successful
- **Specialization:** Combined ships excel in specific roles

### **Counter-Play Options**
**Disruption Tactics:**
- **Interrupt Assembly:** Attack ships during vulnerable assembly phase
- **Forced Separation:** Ion weapons and system damage can break combinations
- **Target Priority:** Focus fire on key components to cripple combined vessel
- **Mobility Denial:** Prevent ships from getting close enough to combine

### **Upgrade Interactions**
**Combination-Specific Upgrades:**
- **Docking Clamps:** Reduce assembly time and separation damage
- **Shared Systems:** Allow combined ships to share all upgrades
- **Emergency Separation:** Allow instant separation without penalties
- **Fusion Protocols:** Enhanced benefits from specific combinations

This system adds tremendous tactical depth through timing, positioning, and resource management decisions. Players must weigh the power of combinations against the vulnerability of the assembly process, creating exciting risk/reward scenarios that can swing battles dramatically.
