# Evolution Branching Paths & Role Transitions
## **Complex Evolution Trees with Role Changes and Specialization**

---

## **EVOLUTION PHILOSOPHY**

### **Three Types of Evolution**
1. **Role Progression:** Ship becomes better at same role (Light Fighter → Heavy Fighter)
2. **Role Specialization:** Ship changes role entirely (Fighter → Interceptor, Bomber → Support)
3. **Multi-Role Evolution:** Ship gains secondary capabilities while keeping primary role

### **Player Choice Impact**
- **Combat Performance** influences available evolution paths
- **Mission Types** unlock role-specific evolutions  
- **Faction Doctrine** affects evolution options
- **Player Playstyle** determines optimal choices

## **ROLE TRANSITION MECHANICS**

### **Evolution Requirements by Role Change**

**Fighter → Interceptor:**
- **Requirement:** 200+ evasive maneuvers, 50+ successful arc dodges
- **Stat Changes:** +1 Agility, -1 Hull, +Boost/Barrel Roll actions
- **New Focus:** Speed and arc-dodging over durability

**Fighter → Bomber:**
- **Requirement:** 100+ structure damage dealt, 25+ capital ship kills
- **Stat Changes:** +1 Hull, -1 Agility, +Ordnance slots
- **New Focus:** Anti-capital and structure damage

**Bomber → Heavy Fighter:**
- **Requirement:** 150+ fighter kills with bomber, 75+ survival missions
- **Stat Changes:** -Ordnance slots, +Maneuverability, +System slots
- **New Focus:** Versatile combat over specialized bombing

**Fighter → Support:**
- **Requirement:** 100+ coordinate actions, 50+ ally saves
- **Stat Changes:** +Coordinate action, +Crew slots, +Defensive focus
- **New Focus:** Team support over individual combat

## **EVOLUTION CHOICE CONSEQUENCES**

### **Permanent Decisions**
Some evolution choices lock out other paths:

**Example: Storm Fighter Choice**
```
If Storm → Talon (Combat Path):
✓ Can become Raptor Interceptor or Vanguard Heavy
✗ Cannot access Hawk Electronic Warfare line
✗ Cannot access Support/Command roles

If Storm → Hawk Electronic (Support Path):
✓ Can become Reaper Support or advanced EW
✗ Cannot access heavy combat roles
✗ Cannot access interceptor line
```

### **Reversible Specializations**
Some evolutions can be modified later:

**Example: Equipment-Based Roles**
```
Hybrid Fighter with:
├── Bio-Combat Package → Heavy Fighter role
├── Psionic Package → Support role (reversible)
└── Adaptation Package → Self-Repair Fighter (reversible)
```

### **Ultimate Evolution Unlocks**
Reaching final evolution tiers unlocks unique capabilities:

**Legendary Ship Abilities:**
- **Aegis Superiority:** "Formation Master" - All friendly ships gain formation bonuses
- **Nova Advanced:** "Inspiration" - Destroyed ship respawns as basic fighter
- **Fortress Heavy:** "Mobile Base" - Can repair and rearm other ships
- **Leviathan Bio-Cruiser:** "Evolution Chamber" - Can upgrade other bio-ships mid-battle

This evolution system creates meaningful long-term progression where every choice matters. Players must consider not just immediate combat effectiveness, but their long-term strategic goals and preferred playstyles. The role-changing evolutions add strategic depth while maintaining faction identity and thematic consistency.
