# Ship Evolution & Campaign Systems
## **"Shattered Stars: Tactical Evolution Framework"**

## **SHIP EVOLUTION TREES**
*Inspired by Tactical Disciples - Ships evolve through battlefield experience*

### **Evolution Philosophy**
Ships gain experience through combat and can evolve along different paths based on player choices, reflecting different tactical doctrines and technological developments. Each evolution maintains the ship's core identity while adding specialized capabilities.

## **EVOLUTION MECHANICS**

### **Experience System**
**Ships gain XP through:**
- **Combat Actions:** 10 XP per enemy destroyed, 5 XP per assist
- **Mission Objectives:** 25 XP per completed objective
- **Survival Bonus:** 15 XP for surviving entire mission
- **Role Performance:** Bonus XP for fulfilling ship role (bomber destroying structures, interceptor killing fighters)

### **Evolution Requirements**
**Base Evolution (Tier 1 → Tier 2):**
- **100 XP** with base ship
- **Complete faction-specific challenge** (varies by evolution path)
- **Player choice** of evolution direction at unlock

**Advanced Evolution (Tier 2 → Tier 3):**
- **250 XP** with Tier 2 ship
- **Master pilot achievement** (specific skill demonstration)
- **Resource investment** (campaign resources/upgrades)

### **Evolution Benefits**
**Stat Improvements:**
- **+1 to primary stats** (Hull, Shields, Attack, or Agility)
- **New action options** (Boost, Barrel Roll, special actions)
- **Additional upgrade slots** (weapons, systems, modifications)
- **Unique abilities** (faction-specific special rules)

**Visual Evolution:**
- **Progressive model changes** (additional armor, weapons, modifications)
- **Color scheme variations** (veteran markings, unit insignia)
- **Weapon effect upgrades** (enhanced particle effects, new sounds)

## **CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE**

### **Act I: The Convergence** *(Tutorial & Introduction)*
**Missions 1-5:** Learn basic ship mechanics with starter fighters
- **Mission 1:** Basic flight training (single faction tutorial)
- **Mission 2:** First combat engagement (AI opponents)
- **Mission 3:** Formation flying and teamwork
- **Mission 4:** First ship evolution choice
- **Mission 5:** Multi-faction encounter (introduce enemy factions)

### **Act II: Rising Tensions** *(Ship Evolution Focus)*
**Missions 6-15:** Unlock evolution paths and explore faction conflicts
- **Faction-specific story arcs** for each of the 5 factions
- **Evolution challenges** unlock new ship variants
- **Territory control** missions introduce strategic layer
- **First epic ship encounters** (boss battles)

### **Act III: Galactic War** *(Advanced Ships & Epic Battles)*
**Missions 16-25:** Full faction warfare with evolved fleets
- **Large-scale battles** using evolved ship varieties
- **Epic ship command** missions (player controls capital ships)
- **Faction alliance/betrayal** story choices affect available ships
- **Final confrontation** using faction's ultimate evolved ships

### **Act IV: Ascendancy** *(Endgame Content)*
**Missions 26+:** Procedural conflicts and ultimate challenges
- **Faction mastery** challenges unlock ultimate evolutions
- **Endless war mode** with progressive difficulty
- **Player-created scenarios** using full ship roster
- **Cross-faction hybrid** ships (earned through special achievements)

## **PROGRESSION UNLOCKS**

### **Ship Availability Timeline**
```
Campaign Start: Basic fighters only (1 per faction)
After Mission 4: First evolution choices unlock
After Mission 8: Medium ships become available
After Mission 12: Large ships enter play
After Mission 16: Epic ships as allies/enemies
After Mission 20: Advanced evolutions unlock
After Campaign: All ships available for skirmish mode
```

### **Evolution Choice Points**
**Players choose evolution paths at key moments:**
- **Mission 4:** First fighter evolution (sets tactical doctrine)
- **Mission 8:** Bomber/support evolution (defines role preference)  
- **Mission 12:** Capital ship selection (faction specialization)
- **Mission 16:** Elite evolution (mastery demonstration)
- **Mission 20:** Ultimate evolution (faction climax choice)

### **Cross-Faction Unlocks**
**Special achievements unlock rival faction ships:**
- **"Know Your Enemy":** Capture intact enemy ships through special objectives
- **"Defector":** Recruit pilots from other factions (rare story events)
- **"Salvage Expert":** Rebuild destroyed ships using battlefield recovery
- **"Diplomat":** Form temporary alliances granting limited ship access

### **Dynamic Mission Generation**
- **Faction conflict matrix** determines which factions appear in missions
- **Ship availability** based on campaign progress and evolution choices
- **Dynamic difficulty** scales based on player ship evolution level
- **Procedural objectives** adapt to player's preferred ship roles

This evolution system creates meaningful progression choices while maintaining tactical depth. Players develop personal attachment to their ships as they evolve, and the branching paths encourage multiple playthroughs to explore different tactical doctrines.
