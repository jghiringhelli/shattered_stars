This browser cannot run Shattered Stars

Your browser is too old, or its graphics (WebGL / Canvas) are turned off.

Minimum versions: Chrome 90, Edge 90, Firefox 90, Safari 15.

  • Update your browser, or open the game in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
  • Turn on hardware acceleration in the browser settings (System) and restart it.
  • Use a desktop or laptop: phones are not supported in this beta.

Best on a desktop browser
